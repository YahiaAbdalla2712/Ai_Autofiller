# FACTORING COMPANY — BUSINESS RULES AND POLICIES

Document Type: Internal Business Rules
Version: 1.0
Purpose: This document contains business rules used when evaluating and processing factoring transactions.

1. CLIENT ELIGIBILITY

---

A client must be an officially registered business before entering into a factoring agreement.

The minimum annual revenue required for standard factoring is 1,000,000 EGP.

Businesses with annual revenue below 1,000,000 EGP require management approval before factoring can be offered.

A client must have been operating for at least 12 months to qualify for standard factoring.

Startups operating for less than 12 months require special approval from the Risk Department.

2. INVOICE ELIGIBILITY

---

Invoices submitted for factoring must be valid commercial invoices issued to an approved debtor.

The minimum invoice value eligible for factoring is 50,000 EGP.

Invoices below 50,000 EGP require manual approval.

An invoice must have a payment due date within 180 days from the invoice submission date.

Invoices with a payment period longer than 180 days require Risk Department approval.

Invoices that are already overdue are not eligible for standard factoring.

An invoice must not have been previously assigned to another financial institution.

3. ADVANCE RATE

---

The standard advance rate is 80% of the approved invoice value.

For example, if an approved invoice has a value of 100,000 EGP, the standard initial advance is 80,000 EGP.

The Risk Department may approve an advance rate between 70% and 90% depending on the debtor risk rating.

Advance rates above 90% require executive approval.

4. FACTORING FEES

---

The standard factoring service fee is 2% of the invoice value.

For an invoice worth 100,000 EGP, the standard factoring fee is 2,000 EGP.

Clients with a low-risk profile may receive a reduced service fee between 1% and 1.5%.

Any service fee below 1% requires management approval.

5. DISCOUNT RATE

---

The standard monthly discount rate is 2%.

The discount is calculated based on the outstanding financed amount and the financing period.

The final discount amount depends on the number of days between the financing date and the expected payment date.

High-risk debtors may receive a monthly discount rate of up to 3%.

A discount rate above 3% requires Risk Department approval.

6. DEBTOR RISK CLASSIFICATION

---

Debtors are classified into three categories:

LOW RISK:

* Strong payment history
* No significant payment delays
* Established business relationship
* Standard advance rate of 80% may be applied

MEDIUM RISK:

* Occasional payment delays
* Moderate financial risk
* Advance rate may be reduced to 75%
* Risk Department review is required

HIGH RISK:

* Frequent payment delays
* Significant financial concerns
* Advance rate may be reduced to 70%
* Risk Department approval is mandatory

Debtors classified as prohibited are not eligible for factoring.

7. PAYMENT TERMS

---

Standard payment terms are between 30 and 120 days.

Payment terms between 121 and 180 days require additional risk assessment.

Payment terms exceeding 180 days require Risk Department approval.

Invoices with payment terms longer than 365 days are not eligible for standard factoring.

8. CONCENTRATION LIMITS

---

A single debtor must not represent more than 30% of the total outstanding factoring portfolio for a client.

If a debtor represents more than 30% of the portfolio, the transaction requires Risk Department approval.

No single client may have more than 50% of its outstanding portfolio concentrated in one debtor without executive approval.

9. REQUIRED DOCUMENTS

---

The following documents are required before a factoring transaction can be approved:

* Valid commercial registration
* Tax registration document
* Valid identification documents for authorized representatives
* Original or verified invoice
* Purchase order or contract when applicable
* Bank account information
* Debtor information
* Evidence of delivery or completion of the underlying transaction when required

Missing mandatory documents prevent final transaction approval.

10. APPROVAL AUTHORITY

---

Transactions with a total invoice value below 500,000 EGP can be approved by an authorized factoring officer if all standard requirements are satisfied.

Transactions between 500,000 EGP and 2,000,000 EGP require Senior Risk Officer approval.

Transactions above 2,000,000 EGP require executive approval.

Any transaction involving a high-risk debtor requires Risk Department approval regardless of invoice value.

11. OVERDUE INVOICES

---

An invoice is considered overdue when the debtor has not paid the invoice by its contractual due date.

Invoices that are 1 to 30 days overdue require collection monitoring.

Invoices that are 31 to 60 days overdue require escalation to the Collections Department.

Invoices that are more than 60 days overdue require Risk Department review.

Invoices that are more than 90 days overdue may result in suspension of additional financing for the client.

12. CLIENT LIMIT

---

Each client receives a maximum approved financing limit.

The initial financing limit is determined based on:

* Client financial strength
* Historical transaction volume
* Debtor quality
* Payment history
* Existing outstanding exposure
* Risk classification

A client cannot receive financing that causes its total outstanding exposure to exceed its approved financing limit.

Requests to increase the financing limit require a new risk assessment.

13. TRANSACTION APPROVAL RULE

---

A factoring transaction may be approved only when:

1. The client is eligible.

2. The debtor is approved.

3. The invoice is valid.

4. The invoice has not previously been assigned.

5. Required documents are available.

6. The transaction does not exceed the client's financing limit.

7. The transaction satisfies the applicable debtor concentration limit.

8. The advance rate is within the authorized range.

9. Required risk approvals have been obtained.

10. EXAMPLE TRANSACTIONS

---

Example 1:

Client: ABC Trading
Invoice Value: 100,000 EGP
Debtor Risk: Low
Payment Terms: 60 days
Standard Advance Rate: 80%
Service Fee: 2%

The transaction qualifies for standard factoring assuming all required documents are available.

Example 2:

Client: XYZ Manufacturing
Invoice Value: 40,000 EGP
Debtor Risk: Low
Payment Terms: 60 days

The transaction is below the minimum standard invoice value of 50,000 EGP.

Manual approval is required.

Example 3:

Client: Delta Supplies
Invoice Value: 1,500,000 EGP
Debtor Risk: Medium
Payment Terms: 150 days

The transaction requires additional risk assessment because the payment period exceeds 120 days.

The standard advance rate may be reduced to 75% because the debtor is classified as medium risk.

Example 4:

Client: Global Services
Invoice Value: 3,000,000 EGP
Debtor Risk: Low
Payment Terms: 90 days

The transaction exceeds 2,000,000 EGP and therefore requires executive approval.

15. IMPORTANT BUSINESS RULE

---

The system must never assume that a missing business value is approved.

If information required for approval is missing, the system must request the missing information rather than inventing a value.

For example, if the debtor risk classification is unknown, the system must not automatically classify the debtor as low risk.

The system should request the debtor risk classification or retrieve it from an approved company data source.

16. TERMINOLOGY

---

Client:
The business requesting factoring services.

Debtor:
The customer of the client who is responsible for paying the invoice.

Invoice Value:
The total monetary value of the invoice.

Advance Rate:
The percentage of the invoice value that is initially financed.

Service Fee:
The fee charged for providing the factoring service.

Discount Rate:
The financing cost applied to the outstanding financed amount.

Outstanding Exposure:
The total amount currently financed and not yet settled.

Approved Financing Limit:
The maximum outstanding financing amount authorized for a client.

Risk Department:
The department responsible for evaluating transactions that exceed standard risk parameters.
